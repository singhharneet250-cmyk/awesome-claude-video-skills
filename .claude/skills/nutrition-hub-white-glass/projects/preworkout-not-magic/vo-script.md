A Hinglish recording script timed to the voiced cut, `out/preworkout-not-magic-vo.mp4` (9:16, 40.5 s).
It has 9 lines and about 34 s of speech. Use it to record the voiceover yourself (to replace the cloned lines),
to brief a voice artist, or as a teleprompter. `vo-script.srt` holds the same lines at the same times.

**Tone:** a friendly expert. Calm and sure, never hype. Use the same pace and energy as the label reel.
**Format:** `/` is a short breath, `//` is a longer pause, and **bold** marks the word to stress.

## Line by line

1. **Hook.** This is the most energy in the reel. Land hard on "nahi", then stop clean.
2. **Count it out.** Give each of the three parts its own beat, so "chaar ingredients" lands as the orbs fly out.
3. **Explain, don't sell.** Let "chhupata" carry the point: caffeine hides tiredness, it doesn't make energy.
4. **Reassure.** Say "harmless" warmly. After the pause, slow down a little on "do se chaar hafte".
5. **Simple and visual.** Give a small lift on "pump", which lands on the BIG PUMP stamp.
6. **Spell out A-T-P** as three letters. Say "teen se paanch gram" clearly, because it is the dose.
7. **The one rule people forget.** Lean on "chhe ghante", which lands on the red ✕.
8. **Ask a real question,** with a rising tone. Pause, then say "PRE" on its own, like a keyword.
9. **Sign-off.** This is the warmest and slowest line, with a smile in the voice. The current cut already uses your real recording
   of this line, so re-record it only if you want the whole voiceover from one session.

## Spoken numbers and their sources

Every number you say is also on screen with its source:

- **2–4 weeks** (beta-alanine): ISSN position stand, Trexler et al., 2015.
- **3–5 g a day** (creatine): ISSN position stand, Kreider et al., 2017.
- **1 hour before training** (caffeine): ISSN position stand, Guest et al., 2021.
- **6 hours before bed** (caffeine and sleep): Drake et al., J Clin Sleep Med, 2013.

Don't add numbers while recording. A spoken number without an on-screen source breaks the brand rules.

## Recording tips

- Record on a phone about 15 cm from your mouth, in a quiet room with soft furnishings. A WhatsApp voice note is fine.
- Read each line two or three times, with a one-second gap between takes. Retakes are fine, because the extra takes get cut.
- Don't rush to fit the times above. Read at your natural pace and the animation follows you.
