## Recording tips

- Record on a phone, about 15 cm from the mouth, in a quiet room with soft furnishings. A WhatsApp voice note is fine.
- Leave a one-second pause between lines. Retakes are fine: just say the line again, and the extra take gets cut.
- Keep the same energy as the label reel: friendly and sure, not hype.
