# Nutrition Hub Khanna: video work

This repo holds the owner's video-editing skills. For every video, graphic, caption or sound task, load the
`nutrition-hub-white-glass` skill first. Its **Owner's standing decisions** list (top of its SKILL.md) wins over any
other skill, plugin or default. In short:

1. Logo: the original file only (`.claude/skills/nutrition-hub-white-glass/assets/logo/`), never redrawn or restyled.
   Ask before adding an end card or the logo to an edit (owner, 2026-11-27).
2. Keep the real background; remove it only when the owner asks for that video.
3. Glass: clear, liquid, glossy, a lens; warm-to-cool tint; ink text; no drop shadows, no glow beside words.
   Accent colour: Logo Red #D30204, taken from the H of the logo (it replaced the old orange, 2026-11-27).
4. Gradients on type, fills, icons, glass and captions, in brand colours only; never on the logo, photos or video.
5. Captions in Roman script (Roman Punjabi for the owner's talks), short, beside the head, never over a card.
6. Voice clear and natural. Output full HD at maximum quality, the biggest clean file platforms accept
   (`encode.py`: CRF 2, AAC 320k), -14 LUFS; ask for the original camera file.
7. Always show the plan (timeline drawing and preview stills) first, and render the final video only after the owner's
   OK, for every edit (owner, 2026-11-27); never render or re-render a full film unless asked.
8. Original music and SFX by default; licensed music, AI voice, AI b-roll or anything that spends credits only with the owner's OK.
9. Every number has a unit and a source; no cure claims; supplements carry a varied-diet line.
10. Never remove the installed skills or plugins (`.claude/skills`, `.claude/settings.json`); merge to `main` only on the
    owner's yes; keep the owner's personal details out of outside services. The repo is public.
